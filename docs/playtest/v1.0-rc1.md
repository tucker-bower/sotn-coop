# Playtest: 1.0.0-rc1 (2026-10-27)

Installed on the PC (silent install, Start menu: SOTN Co-op) and on the Thor (updated in place, saves kept). Both report 1.0.0-rc1, so they can play each other. Release notes draft: `docs/releases/v1.0.0.md`.

## Please try (about an hour)
1. **Start-up:** start a game. "Starting the game" shows at once, and the title should come about 5 s sooner than before.
2. **Online, PC + Thor:** host on one, join on the other by room code. Play from your castle for a while: a shared room, a boss together, a cutscene together.
3. **The three bosses the tests can't finish the game's way:** the Lesser Demon, Olrox, Richter (Developer › Debug starts › Bosses). After each, the fight should end normally: the music changes and the way on opens.
4. **Maria in the Center:** enter the Center through its door on her floor. She should talk, give you the Holy Glasses, and give you control back.
5. **The Librarian** (Debug starts › Scenes): walk left. He greets you, then his shop opens; leave it with △ and walk away.
6. **Richter or Maria with the Gravity Boots:** try a high ledge Alucard reaches with his super jump (see the decision in STATUS).
7. **Maria with a Bible:** pick one up (or any subweapon she has no guardian for): she keeps her guardian and gets a heart.
8. **F9** if anything goes wrong: the report lands in `Documents\SOTN Co-op\reports`.

## Not in this build
- Richter and Maria can't use thrown items (TNT, Karma Coin) from their off hand.
- The inverted castle together (1.1).
