# Playtest 3 plan (M6)

**What's being tested:** the player's whole path, from download to playing, on the bundled OpenBIOS, with nothing set up beforehand.

**Get it:** `Emulators\SOTN-Coop-release\SOTN-Coop-setup-<version>.exe`. Later it will come from GitHub releases.

## Steps
1. **Install:** run the installer, keep the defaults (it installs for your user only, no admin), and tick "Start SOTN Co-op".
2. **First run:**
   - **The disc:** pick your SOTN disc image, the `.cue` in `Emulators\SOTN\` or wherever your rip lives. It's checked, then the co-op disc is made next to it.
   - **Controllers:** press a button on player 1's controller, then player 2's.
   - **Debug starts:** these are made automatically (about a minute).
3. **New game:** both of you start a new game and play from the prologue through Slogra and Gaibon (Alchemy Laboratory) and the Doppleganger (Outer Wall). Save at the coffins as you go.
4. **Quit and Continue:** quit (Esc → "Save and go back to the menu"), close the launcher, start it again, and press Continue. Both games should resume at your last save.
5. **Savestates:** open Savestates on the home screen. Check the sizes, try "Go back to a moment", and open the folder.
6. **F9:** press it at least once and type a note. The zip goes to `Documents\SOTN Co-op\reports\`.
