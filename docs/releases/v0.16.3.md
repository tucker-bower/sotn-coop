**SOTN Co-op 0.16.3**

Fixes from the 0.16 playtest reports. Update both players' games: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### Fixed
- **Staying in the pause or equip menu no longer ends the game.** After about 10 seconds in a menu, the app took the game for frozen and sent it back to the main menu, so your partner saw you disconnect.
- **New castles:** an unnamed castle now stops at the game's name entry so you can type its name. The app used to press through the menus for you and name the castle "A".
- **Richter's hits land once per swing in shared rooms**, boss fights and the prologue's Dracula included. They used to land several times, draining a boss in a few swings.
- **Your partner's body stays still while they're in a menu or a cutscene.** It used to walk and jump as they pressed buttons there.
- **Boss music stops when the fight ends** for both players. The player who wasn't running the fight kept hearing it all through the area.
- **Richter and Maria:** an off-hand attack while crouching now swings low.

Everything else is as in [0.16.2](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop/releases/tag/v0.16.2).
