**SOTN Co-op 1.3.1**

Fixes for what players found in 1.3.0's first days. Update every player's game: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below. The launcher also offers the update itself.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### Fixed
- Widescreen: going into a new area (the entrance into the Alchemy Lab) no longer leaves the backgrounds black, with only enemies and players showing.
- A player who ends up inside the floor, falling without moving, is no longer stuck there: after a moment they're put back where they last stood.
- The app no longer closes when a shortcut is used on the first start's Your discs screen.
