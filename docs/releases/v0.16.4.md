**SOTN Co-op 0.16.4**

A fix for Maria and Richter. Update both players' games: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below.

Everything else is as in [0.16.3](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop/releases/tag/v0.16.3).
