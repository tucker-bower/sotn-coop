**SOTN Co-op 1.0.0**

Version 1.0: play Symphony of the Night together, from the prologue to the end of the first castle and beyond, on PC or Android, alone, on the couch or online. This release adds your own controls, a new character select, and settings split into pages. Update both players' games: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below. The launcher also offers the update itself.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### New
- **Your own controls:** Options › Your settings has a page of buttons for each player. Select a button, then press the controller button or key you want. Every controller works the same: Cross is always the bottom button, so Switch and Xbox controllers no longer swap Cross and Circle.
- **Character select:** left and right pick Alucard, Richter or Maria; up and down pick their version (PlayStation or Saturn Richter, PSP or Saturn Maria). The characters on either side show next to yours.
- **Settings in pages:** Castle settings, Options and Your settings show one group per page. Change page with L1 / R1, or Q / E on the keyboard.
- **Choose where it's installed:** the installer always asks for a folder. Pick a new one (an external drive, say) and your castles, saves and discs move there with it.

### Changed
- Simpler, shorter text throughout the launcher, and rewritten help pages.

### Fixed
- A test now confirms that changing armour in the pause menu as Richter no longer freezes the game on unpausing.

Everything else is as in [0.19.0](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop/releases/tag/v0.19.0).
