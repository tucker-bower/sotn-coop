**SOTN Co-op 0.12: the beta of 1.0. The whole first castle together, with Alucard, Richter or Maria.**

This is everything planned for 1.0. It becomes 1.0 once enough of you have played it: **please tell us how it went**, good or bad, in a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop/issues) (F9 in the game saves a bug report you can attach).

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below.

You need your own *Castlevania: Symphony of the Night* (USA) disc image (`.cue` + `.bin`). Step by step: [Getting started](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop#getting-started).

**Android, if you installed a preview before:** this version is signed with the project's own key, so it can't install over a preview. Uninstall the preview first; its saves (in the app's folder) go with it. Every version from now on installs over this one, saves kept.

### New
- **Cutscenes together.** When one of you starts a scene, the other is brought in and you watch it together, from the prologue to the Castle Keep. Whoever finishes the prologue first waits for the other at Death.
- **See your partner fight.** Their weapon swings, subweapons and spells are drawn on your screen too.
- **Off-hand weapons work like Alucard's** for Richter and Maria: every weapon class, with its own timing.
- **Leaving is graceful:** "Your partner left", in the game and the launcher, with a choice to keep playing solo.
- **Starts faster:** the title comes about 5 seconds sooner, and "Starting the game" shows instead of a black screen.

### Fixed
- In a room you share, the second player in couldn't open a red or sealed door until the first went through it.
- Maria's guardians turned into a skull, a shield or nothing after she held a weapon or shield.
- Picking up a Bible as Maria left her nothing to throw. It now keeps her guardian and gives a heart back, like the Cross, Stopwatch, Rebound Stone and Agunea.
- The Librarian's debug start began past his scene.

### Tested
Every first-castle boss together (Alucard + Alucard, Richter + Maria, Alucard + Richter); every first-castle cutscene but one (below); all 72 hand weapons, 9 subweapons, item crashes, relics and familiars, for each character; the 12 bosses back to back in one run. 167 automated tests, plus the long ones before this release.

### Known issues
- **Richter and Maria jump lower than Alucard with the Gravity Boots** (until the Soul of Bat, which lets them climb anywhere): a ledge Alucard reaches by super jump may need the bat relic for them.
- **Maria in the Center** (the Holy Glasses scene) hasn't been checked from every way in.
- Richter and Maria can't use thrown items (TNT, Karma Coin…) from their off hand yet.
- The inverted castle hasn't been played through together yet (1.1).
- Online play can't connect when one side is on a phone hotspot (or a provider with "symmetric" NAT) and the other's router filters by port. The launcher says so. (A fix that needs no server is planned.)
- Neither the installer nor the app is code-signed: your browser, Windows and Android warn about them (the README shows what to tap).
