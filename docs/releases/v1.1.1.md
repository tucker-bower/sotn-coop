**SOTN Co-op 1.1.1**

Fixes from the 1.1.0 playtests: Richter's fight, bug reports, and a full disk, plus a save history you can go back to. Update both players' games: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below. The launcher also offers the update itself.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### New
- **Save history:** in Load castle, press △ (H on the keyboard) on a castle to see every moment the launcher kept (each new area, boss fight and quit) and play from one. Save at a coffin to keep it.
- **Who plays:** Continue and New game from the menu bar, debug starts and save history now ask Solo, Couch co-op or Host online, instead of always starting two players.
- **The game's own buttons:** remapping shows ✕ ○ □ △ as the game draws them.

### Changed
- **Bug reports send reliably:** a report waits until it's sent, one at a time, and is tried again (also next time you start) if the connection drops.
- **The GitHub and Discord links** on the main menu look like links.

### Fixed
- **Richter's fight in the Castle Keep:**
  - Shaft's orb shows for whoever wears the Holy Glasses, also put on mid-fight, and both players fight the same orb toward the same ending.
  - After the orb, both players always reach the inverted castle. One could be left on the stairs short of the portal.
  - Pausing no longer leaves a frozen Richter on screen.
- **A full disk** no longer costs a save or crashes the game: saves are written whole or not at all, and the launcher warns when space runs low. A disc copy that ran out of space used to keep the disk full.
- **Debug starts** play the characters picked in the lobby.
- **Two players with Maria:** her HUD and guardians no longer scramble the other player's HUD.

Everything else is as in [1.1.0](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop/releases/tag/v1.1.0).
