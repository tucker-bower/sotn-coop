**SOTN Co-op 0.16: everything from our 0.14 playtest.**

Buttons and lifts work for both players, the three characters are closer in power, and crashes now report themselves. Your bug reports showed most of what's fixed here.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

You need your own *Castlevania: Symphony of the Night* (USA) disc image (`.cue` + `.bin`). Step by step: [Getting started](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop/blob/main/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md).

### New
- **Skip cutscenes together:** press Start during a cutscene. When both players have, it runs at high speed.
- **New castles skip the title screen** and go straight into the game.
- **Your partner's health:** a bar under your own. You can turn it off in Settings.
- **Spells on the triggers:** an option for Alucard: hold R2 or L2 and press a face button.
- **Tint option:** turn off the partner's blue tint in Settings.
- **Save a savestate at any time** from the Game menu.
- The launcher shows its version.
- **Rules for each castle:** XP, enemy health, boss aggro, PvP and everything else co-op changes can be set per castle, and come up when a castle starts.
- **Crash reports:** if a game crashes or hangs, the app saves a report and offers it in its own window. Nothing is sent unless you say so, and your partner is told.
- **Alucard's health bar:** an option in Settings for Richter and Maria.
- **Poison:** Richter and Maria can now be poisoned, as Alucard can. Stone and curse come later.

### Changed
- **Richter and Maria's damage:** their plain attacks now match Alucard's, at the start and as they grow. Richter was doing nearly three times as much early on. Tell us how it feels.
- **Pausing:** you're safe while paused, and your partner plays on, everywhere, boss fights included.
- **Enemy health:** every enemy has double health with a partner, not only bosses. It's a rule, from 50% to 400%.
- **Richter online:** a guest's whip used to hit on every frame until the other game caught up. Each swing now hits once.
- **Fist weapons for Richter and Maria:** they now show a punch, for both players.

### Fixed
- **Buttons and lifts** now work for the second player.
- **A freeze in the Beelzebub fight,** and possibly in other fights.
- **Stuck joining online,** especially in boss rush.
- **Corrupted pickup icons** on the second player's screen.
- **Sound crackle:** we changed how sound recovers from a slow frame. Tell us if it's better.
- **Karasuman:** your partner is no longer left in the room with no boss.
- **Bosses' doors** no longer hold up a partner being pulled in.
- **XP** could go wrong when your partner's character was on screen.
- **Stuck players:** the camera's limits now follow each player, which fixes standing on nothing below a room and not being able to go down.
- **Red doors:** Richter and Maria no longer fall into the gap in a doorway and loop through the door for ever.
- **Your partner disappearing:** their character could die on your screen only, or vanish when the connection stuttered.
- **Boss fights restarting:** nobody gets teleported into a reload of a fight they're already in, and two players can't pull each other back and forth.
- **The Doppleganger** no longer gets free hits on a player reloading into the room.
- **Item names** show for both players.
- **Dark Metamorphosis and other spells:** a partner can now see them when the other player's character doesn't know them.
- **Reporting a problem** no longer freezes your partner.
- **Coming back to a castle:** a partner who reconnects is brought into the game, not left at the title screen.
- **Joining after Death's scene:** you lose the Alucard set like everyone else.
- **Dying in a boss fight:** the message box has its text.

### Known issues
- **Same character:** we still can't reproduce your partner seeming to copy your animations. If you see it, press F9.
- **A partner's death** can start from the wrong picture for a moment.
- **Android:** the double-speed console hasn't been checked on a real device.
- Some connections, such as phone hotspots, still can't connect. The launcher says so.
- The inverted castle isn't finished yet.
- Neither the installer nor the app is code-signed: your browser, Windows and Android warn about them. The README shows what to tap.
