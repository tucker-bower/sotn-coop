**SOTN Co-op 0.7.1: online that works over the internet, a proper window, and a lobby.**

Download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below. You need your own *Castlevania: Symphony of the Night* (USA) disc image (`.cue` + `.bin`). How to install and play: [README](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop#play-it-windows-10-or-11).

### New
- **Couch or online, chosen first.** Couch co-op opens a lobby: each player presses a button on their controller to join, then presses Cross / A when ready. Richter and Maria are shown, and come in a later version.
- **Online co-op over the internet.**
  - The host gets a room code like `BAT-7K2Q`; the partner types it.
  - Both games find each other through most home routers (no port forwarding), and the launcher tells you why when a network can't connect.
  - The lobby and the status bar show your ping.
- **Smoother online play.** Your partner and the shared enemies move evenly on Wi-Fi and long-distance connections, and online play uses about a third of the bandwidth it did.
- **A real window.**
  - Resize it, maximize it, or go fullscreen (F11).
  - The menu bar has File, Game, View, Controllers and Help.
  - Layouts: automatic, side by side, stacked (vertical monitors), one player, or player 2 in its own window (a second monitor or TV).
  - Pixels: Sharp or Smooth, always at the game's true 4:3 shape.
- **Unplugging a controller** pauses that player's game until a controller's button is pressed.
- **Help:** how to play, online troubleshooting, and the keyboard keys.
- **Boss debug starts (for testing):** Developer › Debug starts › Bosses starts both players outside any of the 12 bosses of the first castle, with the level, gear and relics a player usually has by then.

### Fixed
- Couch: crackling and buzzing in the intro and menus (both games' sound was added together); cutscenes play together, and leaving a cutscene room no longer loops.
- Online codes pointed at the wrong port on most home networks, and the host never opened a way in for its partner.
- Controllers on Windows: a PS5 (DualSense) controller wasn't picked up in the lobby, and the first controller Windows numbers 0 could ready up but never start the game.
- A partner who restarted the launcher had all their progress ignored until both restarted.
- Anyone who learned your address could take over an online session with one packet. Now only someone with the room code can join.

### Known issues
- Online play can't connect when one side is on a phone hotspot (or a provider with "symmetric" NAT) and the other's router filters by port. The launcher says so.
- The installer isn't code-signed: your browser and Windows SmartScreen warn about it (the README shows what to click).
- Moving platforms don't carry your partner yet, and very big rooms share enemies only while your screens overlap.

0.7.1 can't play online with 0.7.0: both players need the same version.
