**SOTN Co-op 0.11.0: castles you can continue with anyone, solo play, Richter and Maria, and Android.**

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below.

You need your own *Castlevania: Symphony of the Night* (USA) disc image (`.cue` + `.bin`). Step by step: [Getting started](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop#getting-started).

### New
- **Castles.** A castle is your save, and anyone can continue it with you: one night solo, the next with a friend, a week later with another.
  - Solo, Couch and Host online each pick a castle from the list (or start a new one). Joining online asks for the room code only.
  - The friend who joined keeps a copy of your castle, and can later play it solo or host it for someone else. Nothing you've played is ever overwritten: a copy you've played since is kept, and the new one is added as "(2)".
- **Solo.** Play alone: bosses have normal HP and you get the game's full XP.
- **One shared level.** With a partner linked, each kill gives half its XP to both of you, so you level up together at about the solo pace.
- **Richter and Maria, from the start.**
  - Each player picks Alucard, Richter or Maria in the lobby, in any pairing, every session. The same save continues with any characters.
  - Their moves unlock with the relics you find: the double jump, the slide and Richter's blade dash each wait for their relic.
  - They level up, wear armor, and can hold a weapon in a hand (○ swings it).
  - Maria needs your own *Dracula X Chronicles* (PSP) `.iso`: Settings › Game files.
- **Android (preview).** Online co-op on a phone or handheld (AYN Thor, Retroid, Odin…), against another phone or a PC. Pick your disc's folder once and the app does the rest. A controller is needed (no touch controls yet).
- **Alucard's spells on the right stick:** flick it to cast a spell you know (Settings › Controls picks which).
- **Online connects more often:**
  - Two players on the same Wi-Fi find each other even without internet access to the room-code server.
  - The lobby says which step it's on ("Waiting for your partner's game", "Found your partner: reaching them") and, when something's wrong, says what.
  - A partner on another version is told at once, by name, which version each of you has.
  - The room code waits up to 5 minutes for your partner.

### Fixed
- After updating, the co-op disc is remade for the new version, once, from your disc (it used to stay the old one).
- A host by room code refused a partner who typed its ip:port (the documented fallback).
- The launcher opens on screen again when the monitor it was last on is gone.
- Online by room code: a game on the same Wi-Fi that wasn't hosting that room could be taken for your partner.

### Known issues
- **Saves from 0.7 aren't listed** in the castle list. They're still on disk, untouched, in `Documents\SOTN Co-op\playthroughs`.
- Both players need 0.11.0: the launcher tells you by name when your partner has another version.
- The Android app is a preview: sound can crackle on slower devices.
- Online play can't connect when one side is on a phone hotspot (or a provider with "symmetric" NAT) and the other's router filters by port. The launcher says so.
- Neither the installer nor the app is code-signed: your browser, Windows and Android warn about them (the README shows what to tap).
