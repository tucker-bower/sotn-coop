**SOTN Co-op 0.17.0**

Randomizer castles, finds that survive a disconnect, and Richter's and Maria's armour. Update both players' games: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### New
- **Play a randomized castle together.** Make a seed on [sotn.io](https://sotn.io) (or bring its `.ppf`), then choose it in Settings › Game files › Randomizer. New castles use it and remember their seed. Both players need the same seed: the app tells you if you don't have it.
- **Castle rules: "Richter and Maria use Alucard's stats"**, on by default. Their armour, defense and resistances now cut the damage they take, as Alucard's do, and STR and INT add to their attacks. "Richter and Maria level up" is now its own rule.
- **"Relics teach Richter and Maria" off** now gives them every move from the start, instead of none.

### Fixed
- **Richter's and Maria's armour does something.** Their gear and level used to make no difference to the damage they took.
- **Items you find reach your partner even after a disconnect**, including items picked up before they joined.
- **An enemy's item drop goes to whoever picks it up**, instead of a copy for each player.
- **Start skips the prologue's opening text**, even in a new castle.
- **The red door opens for Richter** when he's playing with a partner.
- **Richter and Maria can use the telescope.**
- **Closing the pause menu no longer freezes Maria's or Richter's game** when nothing is in their hands.

Everything else is as in [0.16.3](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop/releases/tag/v0.16.3).
