**SOTN Co-op 0.19.0**

Widescreen, a fullscreen that fills your monitor, and a round of fixes. Update both players' games: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below. The launcher also offers the update itself.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### New
- **Widescreen (experimental):** Options › View › Screen shape. 16:9 or 21:9 shows more of the castle around you, with the game's pixels exactly as they are: nothing is stretched. It's off by default (4:3). In co-op, each player picks their own, and enemies stay awake across the wider of your two views.
- **Fullscreen fills your screen:** the game uses the whole monitor. The status line shows over it for a few seconds when it changes.

### Changed
- **Richter's and Maria's gear:** gear with a special effect now works for them too: the Talisman, Ballroom Mask, Fury Plate, Cat-Eye Circlet, gear that absorbs an element, the Heart Broach, Brilliant Mail, and the Staurolite for Richter's holy water.
- **Maria's attacks:** her guardians grow stronger with INT, and her owl, slide kick, blade dash and high jump with STR. With Gas Cloud, her turtle's crash poisons again.

### Fixed
- **Dying alone** no longer drops you at the game's title screen: your castle's last save loads.
- **Randomizer, 2nd-castle start:** teleporting to a partner on the entrance's trap door lands next to them, not in the inverted castle.
- **Online:** a game stops sending to a partner who has left.

### Known issues with widescreen
- The Game Over's picture is partly garbled.
- In a few rooms and boss fights, something the game keeps just off its 4:3 screen can show near the edges, or an enemy can appear near an edge.

Everything else is as in [0.18.0](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop/releases/tag/v0.18.0).
