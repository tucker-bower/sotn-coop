**SOTN Co-op 1.4.10**

A fix from your bug reports. Update every player's game: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below. The launcher also offers the update itself.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### Fixed
- The launcher could close when you opened a mod preset's Rename / Remove menu with the mouse. It no longer does, there or on any other menu.
- Bug reports now say which mods your disc has, so problems with a randomizer or other mods are quicker to track down.
