**SOTN Co-op 1.4.8**

Clearer Richter and Maria moves. Update every player's game: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below. The launcher also offers the update itself.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### Changed
- Richter's and Maria's moves are unlocked by Alucard's relics (a castle rule, on by default), and the game now says so:
  - When you pick Richter or Maria, the character select reminds you.
  - When you find a relic (or your partner does) that unlocks one of their moves, the game names it: "Blade dash unlocked", "Slide unlocked", and so on.
  - The castle rule's description is clearer.
