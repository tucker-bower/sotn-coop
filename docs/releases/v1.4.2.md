**SOTN Co-op 1.4.2**

Fixes from your bug reports. Update every player's game: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below. The launcher also offers the update itself.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### Fixed
- Two players in one room but on different screens: doors, relics and enemies near the second player no longer go missing or freeze, and that player's hits land again. This could block the way on.
- A dropped item or a relic no longer shows the wrong picture ("NOW MAKE", or a sword) after a menu or a save.
- "Can't reach the room-code server" on some Windows PCs: room codes now work there.
- Online, you can skip a scene your partner isn't in, such as the Prologue while they're already in the castle.

### Bug reports
- Press F9 in the launcher's menus too, not just in a game, to send us a report with the launcher's log.
- Reports are smaller and quicker to send, and a big one is no longer too big to email.
- A report sent from the pause menu no longer shows your room code or IP address in its picture.
