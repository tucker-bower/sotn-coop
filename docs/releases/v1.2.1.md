**SOTN Co-op 1.2.1**

A fix for Android. Update both players' games: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below. The launcher also offers the update itself.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### Fixed
- **Android:** a new install no longer closes itself on its first screen (Your discs).

Everything else is as in [1.2.0](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop/releases/tag/v1.2.0).
