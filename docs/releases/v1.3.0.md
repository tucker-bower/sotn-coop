**SOTN Co-op 1.3.0**

Up to four players, and a batch of fixes. Update every player's game: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below. The launcher also offers the update itself.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### New
- **Up to four players online.** In a new castle's settings, set **Max players** to 3 or 4. Anyone with the room code can then drop in while you play, and each player keeps their own save slot in the castle. Castles stay at two unless you change it.
- **Streamer mode** (Options): room codes and addresses never show on screen. The code is still copied for you to send.
- **The map says what ✕ does:** "✕ Go to partner".
- **Richter and Maria guard with a shield**, as Alucard does.
- **Richter and Maria get curse and stone**, as Alucard does. Cursed, a hand holding a weapon does nothing (potions and Uncurse still work). Stoned, mash buttons to break free.

### Fixed
- Richter's and Maria's hand weapons no longer out-damage Alucard's: they swing at his pace.
- A boss's Max Up no longer counts twice.
- The partner's hits no longer show an HP number flying to your HP meter.
- Richter and Maria no longer get stuck on the Alchemy Lab elevator, and a lift no longer leaves a partner behind on lag.
- In a shared room, the boss's and enemies' sounds no longer go missing for one of you.
- The partner no longer faces the wrong way on your screen, throwing sub-weapons backwards.
- The castle entrance's lightning no longer draws as a grey box when the room is shared.
- Android: the app no longer closes itself now and then as it starts.
