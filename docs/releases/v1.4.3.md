**SOTN Co-op 1.4.3**

Fixes from your bug reports. Update every player's game: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below. The launcher also offers the update itself.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### Fixed
- Widescreen: rooms could lose their whole background and platforms, showing only the characters (seen in the Alchemy Laboratory).
- Revived onto your partner while they stood on a lift: you no longer fall down the empty shaft over and over.

### Bug reports
- Reports now say which screen shape and couch layout you play with.
