**SOTN Co-op 1.1.0**

Fixes from 1.0 playtests: endings, boss fights, bat carry and widescreen, plus easier typing and one-press updates. Update both players' games: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below. The launcher also offers the update itself.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### New
- **Typing works like everywhere else:** in a castle's name, a room code or a bug report, Left and Right move the cursor, and holding Backspace keeps deleting.
- **Richter's backflips** go the way you hold the stick, so you can change direction mid-chain.

### Changed
- **Updating takes one press:** Update now downloads the new version and installs it, with no second screen.
- **Alucard's spells on the right stick** are each player's own setting now, in Your settings beside the spells themselves, instead of a castle setting.
- **Character select** no longer lists each character's shortcuts under them: they're optional.

### Fixed
- **Endings:** every ending plays through to the end for both players. One game used to freeze on the ending's first line.
- **Hanging onto the bat:** each player now sees the other right. The bat no longer turns into a torn Alucard after a door, and the player hanging on no longer appears to walk along the floor.
- **Now Loading** is clean when you continue a castle, with no leftover title screen or scrambled graphics behind it.
- **Boss arenas:**
  - A partner locked out of a boss arena (the Trio) is brought in beside the other player.
  - Nobody is pulled out of their own boss fight into their partner's.
  - A boss fight now carries on correctly when the game running it hands it to the other game.
- **21:9 widescreen:** red doors and enemies at the edges of the screen no longer vanish.
- **Maria:** Mist Form on her forward dash can no longer leave her stuck.

Everything else is as in [1.0.0](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop/releases/tag/v1.0.0).
