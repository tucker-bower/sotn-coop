**SOTN Co-op 1.4.1**

Fixes from your bug reports. Update every player's game: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below. The launcher also offers the update itself.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### Fixed
- Two players as the same character: your partner no longer moves as you do. They're shown with their own moves.
- Ultimate Richter, Ultimate Maria and Saturn Maria no longer turn a weapon's colours, in a pink box, after a weapon is equipped.
- A player joining a room no longer gets "The room is full" for the room they've just joined.
