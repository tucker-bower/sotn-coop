**SOTN Co-op 1.4.5**

Fixes from your bug reports. Update every player's game: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below. The launcher also offers the update itself.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### Fixed
- Coming back after leaving before the first save room no longer means playing the Prologue again: if your partner is already in the castle, it ends at once (you keep the no-damage bonus), and you join your partner.
- Coming back to a game catches you up on what your partner did meanwhile: relics, your shared level, a boss's Life Max Up, and Death taking your gear. Back in a new game, you also get the shared gold.
