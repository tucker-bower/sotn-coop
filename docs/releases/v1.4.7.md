**SOTN Co-op 1.4.7**

Fixes from your bug reports. Update every player's game: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below. The launcher also offers the update itself.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### Fixed
- A game could freeze when your partner dropped food from their hand (a Pot Roast, say) while you held the same food.
- Ultimate Richter's game froze when he switched on a familiar card. Familiar cards now stay off for Ultimate Richter, Saturn Maria and Ultimate Maria, whose own games never had familiars.
