**SOTN Co-op 0.13: your partner is now a real second player, plus PvP and boss rush.**

**This version is relatively untested.** It changes how co-op works underneath, and it hasn't had a full playthrough or a real-world online test. If something feels off, especially online, [v0.12.0](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop/releases/tag/v0.12.0) may be more stable or feel better. Please tell us how it goes: F9 in the game reports a problem.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over 0.12, and your saves stay.

You need your own *Castlevania: Symphony of the Night* (USA) disc image (`.cue` + `.bin`). Step by step: [Getting started](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop#getting-started).

### New
- **Your partner is a real second player** in your game, with their own character, attacks, familiar and sounds, in every pairing of Alucard, Richter and Maria.
- **Boss rush:** all 22 bosses back to back, timed with splits and a best time, solo, on the couch or online.
- **PvP:** an optional mode the host can turn on. It's off by default.
- **Faerie revive:** Alucard's faerie and a Life Apple revive a fallen partner on the spot.
- **Test castles:** start right outside any boss or story scene, with no setup.
- **Easier connecting:** the game now asks your router to open its port, so more pairs of players can connect directly.
- **Extra jumps:** Richter and Maria now get extra jumps instead of higher ones, so they can reach everywhere Alucard can.
- **Bug reports:** after F9, you can send the report to us from inside the game.

### Changed
- You name a new castle when you make it.
- The Join box only pastes a connect code from the clipboard, never anything else.
- Richter dies for both players, so the endings stay in sync.
- A shared boss fight stays shared however far apart you stand.
- Smooth pixels is gone.

### Fixed
- Continuing from a title screen left idle could squeeze the game into the left half of the screen.

### Known issues
- **Karasuman:** in his fight, your partner can be left in the room with no boss, and you fight on alone.
- **Joining online on a slow device:** it can get stuck at the start, especially in boss rush.
- **Maria in the Center:** her Holy Glasses scene ends early, without the Holy Glasses.
- **Richter's name box** on the HUD is blank after loading a save.
- **Android:** sound may crackle; it hasn't been checked on a real device yet.
- Some connections, such as phone hotspots, still can't connect. The launcher says so.
- The inverted castle isn't finished yet (1.1).
- Neither the installer nor the app is code-signed: your browser, Windows and Android warn about them. The README shows what to tap.
