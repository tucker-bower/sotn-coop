**SOTN Co-op 1.4.0**

Online play that keeps up with you: your partner is shown where they really are, and you can hear them. Update every player's game: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below. The launcher also offers the update itself.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### New
- **Your partner on time, online.** Your game now runs your partner a little ahead on a guess of their buttons, and corrects it the moment their real buttons arrive (rollback, like fighting games). They're shown where they are now, not a moment ago.
- **Your partner's swings with their body.** An Alucard partner's sword now swings with his body, in step with his own game.
- **Hear your partner.** Richter's whip and voice (and Maria's, and Alucard's) are heard in the other character's game.
- **Join by address.** While you host, the pause menu and Help › About show your address, for a partner to join without a room code.

### Fixed
- A partner's hits no longer land on their character a moment late, or at the wrong time.
- Enemies in a room you share online no longer freeze and jump on a slow connection.
- A player joining a full room is no longer left waiting: they're told the room is full, and how many players it takes.
