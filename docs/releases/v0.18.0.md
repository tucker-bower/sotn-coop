**SOTN Co-op 0.18.0**

A new launcher, mods in one place, and updates that install themselves. Update both players' games: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below. Choose where to install it: everything lives in that one folder. Your castles and saves move in from Documents the first time you start it.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### New
- **A new launcher in the game's own style.** Its menus, music and sounds come from your own disc, and each of your castles shows the save icon the game gave it.
- **Discs:** drag and drop your disc images onto the window. The PSP and Saturn discs unlock more characters.
- **Mods:** build a mod preset and pick it when you start a castle. Roll a randomized castle in one click (choose a preset, the seed is random, or type one), add any of SOTN Editor's hacks we've tested with co-op, or Alternate Scarlet Symphony -Final-. You and your partner need the same preset.
- **Characters:** Alucard, Richter, Maria (PSP), Maria (Saturn) and Richter (Saturn), each with their portrait.
- **Difficulty:** Easy, Medium, Hard or Nightmare, in a castle's settings. They set enemy HP, damage taken and XP.
- **Boss rush** is a mode you pick for a new castle.
- **Your settings** (sounds, health bars, shortcuts, Alucard's right-stick spells) are in the pause menu, apart from the castle's rules.
- **Updates install themselves:** when a new version is out, the launcher offers it, and your castles and saves stay.
- **Everything in one folder**, wherever you install it. Move the folder and it all comes along.
- **Join our Discord** to find people to play with: it's on the main menu.

### Changed
- **Damage dealt** is gone from the castle rules: harder settings give enemies more HP instead.
- **Alucard's spells on the right stick** is now a castle rule.

### Fixed
- **A bad packet from the network no longer stops your game.**

Everything else is as in [0.17.0](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop/releases/tag/v0.17.0).
