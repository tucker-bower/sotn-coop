**SOTN Co-op 0.16.1**

Fixes for Android. If you play on a PC, you don't need this update.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### Fixed
- **Android:** starting a game crashed the app if you had chosen the Dracula X Chronicles disc for Maria. This had been broken since 0.13.
- **Android:** reporting a problem (F9, or after a crash) closed the app instead of saving the report.
- **Android sound:** no more crackle. Sound used to cut out every few seconds.
- **Android speed:** the game ran 4% too fast. It now runs at the right speed.

Everything else is as in [0.16.0](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop/releases/tag/v0.16.0).
