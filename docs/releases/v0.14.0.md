**SOTN Co-op 0.14: online co-op fixes from our first real playtest of 0.13.**

Our online playtest of 0.13 turned up freezes, slowdown and a door that could lock a player out. This version fixes the ones we could find. There are likely more, so please press F9 the moment anything looks wrong. Both players should be on 0.14. [v0.12.0](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop/releases/tag/v0.12.0) stays available if you'd rather play that.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over 0.13, and your saves stay.

You need your own *Castlevania: Symphony of the Night* (USA) disc image (`.cue` + `.bin`). Step by step: [Getting started](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop#getting-started).

### New
- **Send bug reports from the game:** F9 can now email your report to us directly.
- **Pause menu:** Richter and Maria show their own name and portrait.

### Changed
- **Smoother co-op:** the emulated console now runs at double speed, so your partner no longer drags the game down. Rooms that slowed down on the original PlayStation don't slow down now either.
- Your partner is only tinted blue when you're both playing the same character.

### Fixed
- **Freezes:** a game could stop for good when a room passed from one player to the other, or from losing Alucard's afterimages.
- **The castle door:** the second player through the forest could be locked out at the courtyard gate.
- **Off-hand weapons:** Richter's and Maria's now draw in their own colours.

### Known issues
- **We expect more bugs online.** Reported but not fixed yet: buttons and elevators in shared rooms, corrupted pickup graphics, Dracula in the floor in the opening fight, and red doors looping you through loading.
- **Richter** isn't affected by poison and other status effects.
- **F9** only pauses the player who pressed it.
- **Dying during your partner's boss fight:** you wait on a black screen with an empty message box.
- **Karasuman:** in his fight, your partner can be left in the room with no boss, and you fight on alone.
- **Joining online on a slow device:** it can get stuck at the start, especially in boss rush.
- **Android:** sound may crackle, and the double-speed console hasn't been checked on a real device.
- Some connections, such as phone hotspots, still can't connect. The launcher says so.
- The inverted castle isn't finished yet (1.1).
- Neither the installer nor the app is code-signed: your browser, Windows and Android warn about them. The README shows what to tap.
