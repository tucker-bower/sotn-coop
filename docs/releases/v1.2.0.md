**SOTN Co-op 1.2.0**

Richter and Maria hold weapons in both hands, like Alucard. This version also fixes bugs from the 1.1.1 playtest. Update both players' games: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below. The launcher also offers the update itself.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### New
- **Richter's and Maria's hands work like Alucard's:** □ uses the first hand and ○ the second. With the first hand empty (or holding a shield), □ is still the whip or the owl. Put a sword there and □ swings the sword. A two-handed weapon swings on both buttons. ↑+□ still throws the subweapon.
- **Bug reports (F9) show more:** each report adds a picture of every hitbox on screen and what last hurt each player.

### Changed
- **The status page's ATK** for Richter and Maria shows what each button really hits for.

### Fixed
- **A crash at Doppleganger10** when the boss's reward appeared.
- **The partner's Richter** no longer jumps around on your screen while he runs.
- **Warp rooms:** either player can warp, and the player left behind no longer sees a white screen.
- **Richter's and Maria's familiar list** shows the familiars' pictures.
- **Widescreen** no longer shakes near a room's edge.

Everything else is as in [1.1.1](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop/releases/tag/v1.1.1).
