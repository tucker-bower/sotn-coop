**SOTN Co-op 0.16.2**

A fix for continuing a castle. Update both players' games: partners need the same version to play online.

- **On a PC:** download **SOTN-Coop-setup.exe** below.
- **On an Android phone or handheld:** download **SOTN-Coop.apk** below. It installs over the last version, and your saves stay.

### Fixed
- **Continuing a castle made you Alucard.** Playing solo or online, Continue started you as Alucard whoever you had picked. You now continue as Richter or Maria. Your progress was never lost: it's all still there.
- **A game could freeze for good** when your partner was pulled to you while their game was still loading a room.
- **A game could crash** when both players walked into a boss in the inverted castle at once.
- **Arriving next to your partner** no longer drops you into a doorway.
- **Android:** starting a game crashed the app if you had chosen the Dracula X Chronicles disc for Maria. This had been broken since 0.13.
- **Android:** reporting a problem (F9, or after a crash) closed the app instead of saving the report.
- **Android sound:** no more crackle. Sound used to cut out every few seconds.
- **Android speed:** the game ran 4% too fast. It now runs at the right speed.

### Changed
- **Online:** your partner's moves reach you about twice as fast (67 ms instead of 133 on our test connection).
- **Settings › Show ping:** the round trip to your partner, top right while you play online.
- **Online:** connecting works through more home routers.

Everything else is as in [0.16.0](https://github.com/tucker-bower/sotn-coop/releases/tag/v0.16.0).
