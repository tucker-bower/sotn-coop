# SOTN Co-op playtest

Double-click a numbered `.bat` file. Both games open side by side: the **left** game is the first controller, the **right** game the second (or the keyboard: the controls are in the window's bottom bar). **F1** switches which game you hear. **ESC** quits; memory cards are kept in `cards\`.

| # | Preset | Try this | Tell me |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shared room fight | Walk left into the hall together and fight the zombies and the Warg. The bar says SHARED ROOM. | Same enemies on both screens? Both players' hits count? Do enemies go for whoever's closer? Anything flicker, vanish or look wrong on the right screen? |
| 2 | Teleport and respawn | Right player: SELECT (map), then ✕, to jump to the left player. Then the other way. Then let one player die. | Did you land next to your partner? After dying, did you reappear by your partner with full HP? |
| 3 | Save room | Left player: step into the coffin, press UP. Then ESC. | Did the right game freeze for a moment, then carry on? Was the left player healed and the right not? |
| 4 | Resume from Load menu | In each game: START, ✕ (File select), ✕ on the save. | Do you both start in the save room, each with your own HP and items? |
| 5 | Explore apart | Pick things up, break things, open the map (SELECT). | Does your partner get what you find? Does the map show your partner's marker and rooms? |
| 6 | New game from scratch | Full start: new game in both, play as far as you like. | Anything weird, anywhere. |

**Known gaps (no need to report):** a heart picked up by the right-hand player in a shared room goes to the left-hand one; moving platforms don't carry the guest player; there's no on-screen co-op text yet (teleport plays the confirm or error sound).

Notes like "#1: at 2:10 the Warg on the right screen jumped" are perfect. A phone video helps too.
